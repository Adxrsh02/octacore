# 1. BUSINESS OVERVIEW

## Brand Name
OCTACORE Fitness

## Business Category
Gym / Physical Fitness Center / Fitness & Training Facility

## Business Description

OCTACORE Fitness is a fitness brand operating across two locations in Navi Mumbai: Nerul and Airoli.

Built around the philosophy of FITNESS • PASSION • SPIRIT, OCTACORE represents a training environment focused on physical development, consistency, dedication and personal fitness journeys.

The brand aims to serve individuals with different fitness goals, from beginners taking their first steps into fitness to experienced individuals committed to improving their strength, endurance and physical performance.

With two conveniently located fitness facilities, OCTACORE brings its fitness community closer to residents and working professionals in Nerul and Airoli.

## Core Brand Philosophy

FITNESS — Develop a stronger and healthier lifestyle.

PASSION — Approach every workout with dedication and commitment.

SPIRIT — Build the mindset, confidence and consistency required to progress.

## Brand Positioning
