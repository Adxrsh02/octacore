## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-performance, cinematic visual experience inspired by the precision of luxury automotive engineering and the raw intensity of elite athletic training. It targets high-performing athletes, executives, and fitness enthusiasts who expect their digital experience to match the tactile sophistication of a bespoke performance facility.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Cinematic Dark Minimalism** with **Precision Engineering**:

- **Monolithic Darkness**: Deep, void-like blacks form an unyielding foundation, eliminating clutter and directing laser focus toward human performance and kinetic metrics.
- **Atmospheric Rim Lighting**: High-intensity performance orange and amber gold function like instrument cluster backlights and automotive brake-caliper accents, cutting through shadow with high-energy urgency.
- **Architectural Rigor**: Clean geometry, controlled 16px corner radii, and hyper-subtle optical glass borders suggest milled aerospace aluminum, carbon composite panels, and smoked glass.
- **Visceral Energy**: Visuals and typography evoke focused power, calculated aggression, and relentless forward momentum without resorting to kitsch or superficial neon tropes.

## Colors

The palette operates on absolute dark dominance punctured by high-chroma kinetic accents.

### Color Tiers & Roles

- **Base Canvas (`#080808`)**: The primary deep void background. Absorbs visual noise and anchors high-contrast imagery.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#141414`)**: Secondary background layer used for structural panels, navigation rails, and backdrop separations.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1B1B1B`)**: Elevated card surface. Represents tactile, structural modules.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#242424`)**: Interactive hover states, elevated modals, and segmented controls.
- **Primary Kinetic Accent (`#FF8500`)**: Pure kinetic energy. Reserved strictly for primary callouts, high-priority biometric indicators, active timer states, and core CTAs.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F5A623`)**: Precision amber gold. Utilized for status progressions, secondary metrics, achievement badges, and subtle internal glow edges.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#FF5100`)**: High-intensity combustion orange for maximum heart-rate zones, critical alerts, and dynamic peak-effort states.
- **Primary Text (`#FFFFFF`)**: High-contrast readouts, critical display headings, and primary metrics.
- **Secondary Text (`#A0A0A0`)**: Structural labels, metadata, secondary readouts, and passive body copy.
- **Tertiary Muted Text (`#555555`)**: Disabled indicators, metric units (`BPM`, `KG`, `SEC`), and timestamp watermarks.
- **Structural Border Tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)**: Hairline definition that mimics precision-cut panel seams.
- **Atmospheric Glow Layer (`rgba(255, 133, 0, 0.15)`)**: Sub-surface kinetic scatter for selected active states and progress rings.

## Typography

Typography establishes an uncompromising athletic tension between industrial muscle and Swiss clarity.

- **Display & Headings (Oswald)**: All headings, telemetry markers, and section badges render in strict uppercase. The condensed verticality projects power and urgency while conserving horizontal space for data readouts.
- **Metric Digits (Oswald)**: Timers, reps, kilowatt readouts, and heart rate values utilize bold tabular Oswald numerals for instantaneous glanceability during high-strain workouts.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Inter)**: Body text is neutral, highly legible, and deliberately understated in lower contrast (`#A0A0A0`) to provide breathing room and prevent visual fatigue against the intense headings.
- **Micro-Labels & Technical Data**: Small status flags and category markers employ `label-caps` with wide character tracking (`0.12em`) to recreate cockpit and performance telemetry gauges.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout mimics automotive chassis construction: rigid, proportioned, and responsive to high-stress performance viewing.

### Grid Geometry & Breakpoints

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` (48px) margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Content sections feature structured containment maxing at 1440px to preserve cinematic widescreen framing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. Primary cards collapse from 3-wide to 2-wide; data tables transition into responsive metric stacks.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column fluid grid, `1.25rem` (20px) margins, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Heavy telemetry strips convert into single-column vertical swipe modules with pinned biometric bars.

### Rhythmic Discipline

- Internal card padding is unified at `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) on desktop and `1rem` (`space-md`) on compact mobile frames.
- Negative margins and micro-spacers (`space-xs` = 4px) anchor unit indicators directly adjacent to metric numerals without drift.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on generic drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **Tonal Stratification**, **Frosted Smoked Glass**, and **Atmospheric Kinetic Rim Lighting**.

### Depth Layers

1. **Foundation Level (0dp)**: Absolute `#080808` matte canvas.
2. **Structural Tier (1dp)**: Solid `#141414` panels bounded by a single hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Interactive Metric Panels (2dp)**: Solid `#1B1B1B` card containers. Under hover or active focus, a dynamic hairline gradient illuminates the top edge (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(255,133,0,0.4), transparent)`).
4. **Cinematic Glassmorphism (Floating UI / Navigation)**:
   - Surface: Smoked obsidian glass (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.72)`).
   - Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Border: Hairline boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) on bottom/lateral edges.
5. **Atmospheric Luminescence (The Kinetic Rim)**:
   - Reserved for active session monitors, high-output zones, and primary interactive states.
   - Constructed using an ambient box-shadow: `0 0 40px -10px rgba(255, 133, 0, 0.25)`, paired with a secondary tight inner glow (`inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The design language mandates a controlled, cohesive corner radius across all interactive elements, matching the 16px specification.

- **Standard Cards, Panels & Modals (`rounded-lg`)**: Set strictly to `1rem` (16px). This creates an ergonomic, precision-molded automotive aesthetic that avoids both severe brutalist corners and overly casual rounded forms.
- **Interactive Controls & Buttons (`rounded`)**: Default to `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, inputs, and chips to convey tactile structural resilience.
- **Progress Badges & Status Nodes (`rounded-full`)**: Complete circular geometries are reserved exclusively for avatar containers, live workout state pulses, and dial indicators.

## Components

### 1. Buttons

- **Primary Kinetic Action**:
  - Background: Solid `#FF8500`.
  - Text: `#080808`, `Oswald` Bold, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.08em`.
  - Border: None.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#FF9924` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 133, 0, 0.4)`).
  - Active: Scale transform `0.98`.
- **Secondary Industrial Action**:
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, `Oswald` Medium, uppercase.
  - Hover: Border color turns to `#FF8500`, background to `rgba(255, 133, 0, 0.08)`.

### 2. Metric & Telemetry Cards

- **Structure**: Surface `#1B1B1B`, 16px radius, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Header**: Secondary label caps (`label-caps`) in `#A0A0A0` accompanied by an optional status icon.
- **Hero Data**: Numerals in `metric-md` or `metric-huge` in `#FFFFFF`, flanked by uppercase unit tags (`BPM`, `WATT`, `RPM`) in `#555555`.
- **Bottom Edge Accent**: Real-time graph or mini sparkline tinted `#FF8500` fading downward to transparent.

### 3. Navigation Bar (Smoked Glass Cockpit)

- Fixed floating or pinned top navigation with a 24px backdrop blur and 72% opacity on `#141414`.
- Under-edge demarcated by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Active navigation links feature a bottom indicator: an energetic 2px horizontal pill in `#FF8500` with a subtle orange glow.

### 4. Input Fields

- **Container**: Background `#141414`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, 8px radius.
- **Typography**: Inter 14px (`body-md`), text color `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#555555`.
- **Focus State**: Border transitions to `#FF8500`, accompanied by a subtle outer ring glow `0 0 0 1px #FF8500`.

### 5. Chips & Filters

- **Default**: `#141414` background, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#A0A0A0`, 8px radius.
- **Active / Selected**: Surface turns to `rgba(255, 133, 0, 0.15)`, border snaps to `#FF8500`, text switches to `#FF8500`, weight semi-bold.

### 6. Biometric Progress Rings & Dials

- Inactive track rendered in `#242424` at 6px stroke.
- Active progress ring rendered in `#FF8500` or gradient `#FF8500` to `#F5A623`.
- Terminal cap of stroke features an ultra-bright rim point with an outer glow filter for live telemetry tracking.

### 7. Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)

- Custom square/circle housing with `1.5px` border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on `#141414`.
- Checked state: Interior floods with `#FF8500`, displaying a sharp, contrasting black checkmark or center pip.
